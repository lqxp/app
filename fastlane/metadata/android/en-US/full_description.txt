QxChat is an end-to-end encrypted messenger: neither our servers nor anyone else can read your messages.

• End-to-end encryption on every room and call
• Multi-device sync via QxCloudSync: your rooms and keys follow you everywhere
• Voice calls and collaborative whiteboard
• Built-in Tor mode, client lock, decoy vault, 12-word recovery
• Zero tracking, zero ads, open source code

Your keys stay on your devices. The server only relays encrypted packets it cannot read.

QxChat is free and open source (MIT). Join us at getqxchat.com.
